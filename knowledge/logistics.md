# Logistics And Schedule

## Day one

Check-in starts at 09:00 on June 6 at HOIV. Cases go live at 10:00 and doors close at 11:00 sharp.

Opening ceremony runs from 11:00 to 11:30. Break-out rooms run from 11:30 to 12:30. Teams finalize structure and track preferences from 12:30 to 12:45. Final allocation runs from 12:45 to 13:00, and hacking starts at 13:00.

Lunch is planned from 14:00 to 15:30. Dinner is planned from 18:30 to 22:00. Vegan options are included.

## Day two

Breakfast is planned from 09:00 to 11:00 on June 7. Optional draft submission is at 12:00. Lunch runs from 12:00 to 14:00.

Code freeze and final submission are at 14:00 on June 7. Community Award pitches run from 14:00 to 16:00. Track winners pitch from 16:00 to 17:00. After-event networking runs from 17:00 to 18:00, and the venue closes at 19:00.

Participants should use the event Discord for team formation and organizer questions. On-site staff and partner mentors can clarify track-specific details during the hackathon.
